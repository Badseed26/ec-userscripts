# ec-userscripts

Ethan's Tampermonkey scripts for trade admin. Open an install link in a browser with Tampermonkey and hit Install. Updates come through automatically: each script checks this repo every 2 min and runs a newer version on the next page load (live updates, from 23/10), and Tampermonkey's own update every few hours catches the installed copy up. A change that needs a new permission waits for that Tampermonkey update. The code is in src/, the files here are built from it by build.py.

- AroFlo supplier items 0.40 (AroFlo quotes, PC and iPhone, beta): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Badseed26/ec-userscripts/main/aroflo-supplier-items.user.js
  An orange Supplier button next to each Add Line Item on a quote. It opens a small window (full screen on a phone, sized to what's on screen even on Request Desktop Website or with the keyboard up): search CNW and Tradezone at once with your own sign ins, see the ex GST price, unit and stock at your branch, every result on the supplier's page with Next and Prev to go through their pages one at a time (pick any CNW or Tradezone branch in the window with type to search, defaults Oxenford and Gold Coast), each with a small photo, CNW and Tradezone under their own headings (tap a heading to fold that supplier away, it stays folded next time), set the qty and tap Add. Signed out of a supplier shows as its own box with a Sign in button: with a saved login it signs in in the background (no tab) and searches again; if that doesn't take, or there's no saved login, it opens their sign in page in a new tab, signs in by itself if you've saved that login under Logins (kept in that device's Tampermonkey only, one try so a wrong password can't lock you out) or waits for you to tap your saved password, then closes the tab and the window searches again. AroFlo isn't reloaded, so nothing unsaved on the quote is lost. Needs a Tampermonkey update check once (new CNW and Tradezone matches). Order (in the window, or Order parts on a locked quote) lists every line with a Part No, works out CNW or Tradezone for each, rounds part quantities up, to the supplier's minimum order and lot size where it has one, and merges doubles, shows today's price and branch stock, then puts the ticked ones in that supplier's cart and reads the cart back. It never checks out. The Supplier button inside a folder adds into that folder. Each result shows the same item at the other supplier when the codes line up (forgiving on case, spaces, dashes and the brand letters at the front), marked Same item? with an X to drop a wrong match, and which is cheaper, with Show it (jumps to that one, or pops it in if it's on another page) and Use it instead (adds that one at this card's qty). Out of stock at your branch, it checks the 3 nearest and shows who has it, with Find in stock for a similar product. Star results to keep them up top with your last 10 adds. Reprice rechecks every Part No line against today's price, ticks what's moved, offers a swap to the other supplier when it's cheaper, and changes nothing till Update ticked. Price history (each add and reprice, 12 months) shows as was X on date. Favourites, recents, history and dropped matches sync between devices through the * Supplier items data task in Todoist (token saved under Logins). Also has a bridge the quote skill uses for stage 3 pricing (search, read the lines, put a cost in Value), so pricing runs by script instead of browsing the supplier sites. Copy debug in the window grabs what Claude needs if a supplier comes back empty.

- EC Ledger 0.9 (AroFlo home page, PC and iPhone, beta): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Badseed26/ec-userscripts/main/ec-ledger.user.js
  A teal Ledger button on the AroFlo home page, left of Month pull. The version shows next to the name in its window. It keeps a pretend bank account: hit Refresh and it reads everything again from a month before the quarter before the start date (invoices, purchase orders, timesheets and the STC lines on solar jobs off AroFlo, then the Tradezone and CNW order lists, so sign in to both in the same browser first), and replays it into a statement. In: invoices inc GST on their terms (14 days, or whatever a client is set to), STC money 2 days after the job. Out: Tradezone and CNW accounts 30 days end of month, other suppliers off AroFlo's purchase orders on the day, pays every Wednesday for the Wednesday to Tuesday before (hourly people on a 38 hour guarantee with travel counted, overtime over 8 hours on a weekday and on weekends and public holidays at the award rates, or a flat weekly salary), super with each pay, tax held from pays and GST on the ATO's dates, and the running costs that can't be read from here as editable estimates. No names or pay figures are in the script: they're typed in under Settings, stay in that device's Tampermonkey, and move to another device with a settings code (a full code replaces that device's settings, an add-on code only adds what's in it, e.g. a few people for the pay list). The maths is checked by tools/ec_ledger_test.js (node tools/ec_ledger_test.js).
